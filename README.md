# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Business Analyst Portfolio Project

An end-to-end Business Analyst project focused on analyzing sales and profitability performance using the Superstore dataset.

The project combines business requirements analysis, data exploration, SQL analysis, and Power BI visualization to identify business performance gaps and provide data-driven recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The company has a large volume of sales data but lacks clear visibility into the factors driving profitability.

Management wants to understand which products, categories, regions, customer segments, and discount levels contribute positively or negatively to profit.

The objective of this project was to analyze these areas, identify underperforming segments, and provide actionable insights to support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Identify profitable and underperforming categories
- Compare sales and profit across regions
- Analyze customer segment performance
- Evaluate the relationship between discounts and profitability
- Identify loss-making products and sub-categories
- Analyze sales and profit trends over time
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

## 💡 Key Business Findings

### 1. Technology was the strongest category by profit

Technology generated the highest total profit with an approximate profit margin of **17.40%**.

### 2. Furniture had a profitability problem

Furniture generated approximately **$742K in sales**, but its profit margin was only about **2.49%**.

This indicates that high sales volume did not translate into strong profitability.

### 3. Tables were a major loss-making sub-category

Tables generated approximately **$17.7K in losses**, making them the largest loss-making sub-category in the analysis.

### 4. High discounts were associated with negative profit

Profit became negative at discount levels of **30% and above**.

This highlights the need to evaluate discount strategies carefully.

### 5. Regional performance varied

The **West** region generated approximately **$108.4K in profit** and had a profit margin of approximately **14.94%**.

The **Central** region had the lowest regional profit margin at approximately **7.92%**.

### 6. Customer segment performance differed

The **Consumer** segment generated the highest sales and total profit, while **Home Office** had the highest profit margin.

### 7. Product profitability varied significantly

The **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** was the highest-profit product, while the **Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print** had the largest individual product loss.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following areas should be considered by management:

### 1. Review high-discount transactions

Evaluate discount policies, particularly at higher discount levels, to understand their effect on profitability.

### 2. Investigate loss-making sub-categories

Review pricing, discounting, product costs, and sales strategies for sub-categories such as Tables and Bookcases.

### 3. Review Furniture profitability

Investigate why Furniture generates substantial sales but relatively low profit margins.

### 4. Analyze regional performance

Investigate the factors contributing to differences in regional profitability and identify opportunities to improve weaker regions.

### 5. Monitor product-level profitability

Identify consistently profitable and loss-making products to support pricing and product-management decisions.
