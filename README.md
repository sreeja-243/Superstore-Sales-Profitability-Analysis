# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and profitability performance using the Superstore dataset.

The project was developed from a Business Analyst perspective to help management understand overall business performance, identify profitable and underperforming areas, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

The company has a large volume of sales data but lacks clear visibility into the factors driving profitability.

Management wants to understand which products, categories, regions, customer segments, and discount levels contribute positively or negatively to profit.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Identify profitable and underperforming categories
- Compare regional sales and profitability
- Analyze customer segment performance
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and profitability
- Identify loss-making products and sub-categories
- Analyze sales and profit trends over time

## Tools Used

- Excel
- PostgreSQL
- Power BI
- Business Requirements Documentation

## Project Approach

Business Problem → Requirements → Data Analysis → SQL Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Findings → Recommendations

## Key Business Findings

- Technology generated the highest total profit.
- Furniture generated approximately $742K in sales but had a very low profit margin.
- Tables were the largest loss-making sub-category.
- Bookcases and Supplies were also loss-making sub-categories.
- Profit became negative at discount levels of 30% and above.
- West was the strongest region by total profit.
- Central had the lowest regional profit margin.
- Consumer generated the highest sales and total profit.
- Home Office had the highest profit margin.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest-profit product.
- Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print had the largest individual product loss.
